# Player Blocking, Enemy Attack Animation & Combat Cleanup

Today I continued developing the basic combat loop and cleaned up the existing combat architecture.

### Player Combat & Blocking

Created a dedicated `PlayerCombat` component to handle player combat actions.

The blocking system uses the existing Unity New Input System and `PlayerInputActions`.

Blocking is handled through:

* `Block.IsPressed()`
* `IsBlocking` property

The blocking responsibility was moved out of `PlayerMovement`, keeping movement and combat logic more separated.

`PlayerMovement` now uses `PlayerCombat.IsBlocking` to prevent actions such as sprinting and jumping while blocking.

### Enemy Attack Animation

Integrated the enemy attack with its Animator and attack animation.

The enemy now starts its attack through an Animator Trigger instead of handling the attack entirely through code.

Added animation-event based combat timing:

* `EnableHitbox()` at the moment of sword contact
* `DisableHitbox()` after the active hit window
* `FinishAttack()` at the end of the attack animation

Because the enemy Animator is located on the weapon object, an `EnemyAnimationEvents` component was added there to forward animation events to the appropriate combat and AI components.

This connects the animation directly with the enemy hitbox and attack state.

### Enemy Hitbox

Created `EnemyHit` based on the existing player `SwordHit` system.

The enemy weapon can now detect the player during the active attack window and apply damage through `PlayerHealth`.

The system also checks `PlayerCombat.IsBlocking` before dealing damage.

If the player is blocking, the attack is detected but normal damage is prevented.

A `HashSet` is used to prevent the same attack from damaging the player multiple times.

### Enemy AI Cleanup

Cleaned up `EnemyAI` so that it focuses on AI responsibilities rather than directly handling hit detection and damage.

The AI is responsible for:

* detecting the player,
* chasing the player,
* entering the attack state,
* starting the attack animation,
* waiting for the animation to finish,
* returning to the appropriate state.

Damage and hit detection are now handled by the enemy weapon and its hitbox.

### General Code Cleanup

Started separating responsibilities between the main systems:

* `PlayerMovement` → movement and camera
* `PlayerCombat` → player combat and blocking
* `PlayerHealth` → player health
* `EnemyAI` → enemy behaviour and states
* `EnemyHealth` → enemy health and death
* `EnemyHit` → enemy attack hit detection
* `EnemyAnimationEvents` → animation event communication

This makes the combat system more modular and provides a cleaner foundation for expanding the combat mechanics later.

### Current State

The basic structure of the player blocking system is in place, enemy attacks are connected to their animation, and the combat code has been partially cleaned up and separated into dedicated responsibilities.
